What We Offer

Whether you want to pursue the Dark Core Pet, work on your collection log, improve your kills per hour (KPH), join Corp masses or enjoy other PvM content, our community is here to help.

We offer Corp boosting packages, raids support, God Wars Dungeon activities and organized community events. Contact our team through official Discord tickets to discuss services and availability.

Trusted Leadership & Community

We value teamwork, respectful communication, clear rules and accountability. Our staff and boosters are expected to treat customers fairly and help maintain a welcoming community.

Safe & Responsible Play

WeCorpCC does not offer or support real-world trading (RWT). RWT violates Jagex rules and can result in account penalties, including bans. We aim to operate in accordance with applicable Jagex rules. Players should review the current official rules and ensure their own activities comply.

Become a Booster 💰

Experienced Corp and PvM players can contact our team about joining as boosters and earning in-game GP by providing services. We look for skilled, reliable players who value teamwork and professionalism. Applications are subject to community requirements.

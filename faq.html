BOOST FAQ

Answers to common questions about our boosting teams, loot rules, booster applications, referrals and refunds.

1. Why boost with WeCorpCC?

Unlike services that use one booster on many accounts to solo-boost a client, WeCorpCC groups approximately 7–10 individual players to help boost each client. Boosting with us supports real players working together toward their in-game goals.

2. If a booster snipes a kill and gets a valuable drop, who keeps it?

If a booster snipes a kill by out-DPSing the client, the drop will be traded to the client or left on the ground for them to loot.

If the client was not present for at least 60% of the fight, the boosters keep the loot and split it among themselves. The loot is not FFA unless agreed upon.

3. Can I apply to become a booster?

Yes. If you meet the required boosting gear and combat-level requirements, visit #role-picker in the Boosting section of our Discord and choose the applicable role.

You may also use multiple eligible accounts to fill ticket roles; payment is made per account.

4. Can I bring my alt while getting boosted?

Yes, as long as your alternate account meets the boosting requirements. You may sign up on your own boost using that alt and receive payment for its role, or use it toward a discounted boost.

Existing boosters have priority when signing up, unless there are not enough boosters available.

5. How do I know WeCorpCC services are safe?

WeCorpCC states that its boosting services follow Jagex rules and that the team checks activities for rule compliance. The original FAQ reports more than 1,000 boosted tickets.

For any service, review the current terms and discuss questions with staff before ordering.

6. How do I get ranked?

Participate actively in boosts and community masses. Administrators consider your involvement and contributions when awarding ranks. Splitting valuable loot, including sigils obtained during masses, can also contribute toward rank consideration.

7. How do I report an issue or discuss a concern?

Open a support ticket in the Discord server to contact staff. A team member will assist when available.

8. Someone in the Discord server DMs me promoting a Corp boosting service. What should I do?

Inform a staff member or administrator and ignore the unsolicited message. WeCorpCC boosters should never DM clients to offer boosts. Discuss boosts only through official ticket channels such as #ticket-chat or #request-corp-boost.

9. Do I get anything for referring a friend to WeCorpCC?

According to the supplied referral policy, if you refer a friend who purchases an Elysian Pack+, you receive a one-time 3M GP commission per referred friend who gets boosted, provided they mention your referral before the boost in #request-corp-boost.

For a friend who chooses a Spectral Pack, the original FAQ says the one-time referral commission depends on whether they receive a sigil drop:

Spectral Sigil: 3M GP
Arcane Sigil: 10M GP
Elysian Sigil: 25M GP

10. Do I get a refund if I want to end the trip early?

Refunds depend on the number of kills ordered, the number completed, and when the trip is ended. The supplied policy says a refund may be issued for unfinished kills after 60 KC.

Example from the original FAQ: If you order 100 KC on an Ely Pack and receive the pet at 40 KC, WeCorpCC keeps 20M GP from the remaining payment and refunds 40M GP if you choose to stop early.

Confirm the current refund calculation with staff before purchasing or ending a trip.

These answers reflect the FAQ material provided by WeCorpCC. Referral rewards, loot handling, and refunds should be confirmed with staff before ordering, as policies may change.
